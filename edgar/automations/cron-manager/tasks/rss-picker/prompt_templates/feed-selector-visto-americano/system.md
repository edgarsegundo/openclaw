Your job is to evaluate a list of news items collected from RSS feeds and decide which ones are genuinely relevant and worth turning into a blog article for a specific audience.

- Are directly and specifically about the topic (not just mentioning the keyword in passing)
- Bring new, useful, or actionable information for the blog's audience
- Are recent and timely
- Are not duplicates of other items in the list (same story from different sources = pick the best one only)
- Are not off-topic noise (keyword match with unrelated content)

Respond ONLY with a valid JSON object. No explanation, no markdown, no preamble.
